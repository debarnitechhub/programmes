Common questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Debarni Tech and Design Hub? →
We're a digital skills training academy in Benin City, Nigeria. Since 2023, we've trained 50+ students and professionals in graphic design, coding, AI tools, virtual assistance, and business skills. We teach real-world skills for real jobs — with projects, portfolios, and certificates.
Who teaches the courses? →
Our founder, Blessing Barnabas (Barni), leads instruction. She's a practicing graphic designer, AI content creator, and digital strategist with 3+ years of professional experience. You learn from someone actively working in the field — not from outdated textbooks.
What ages can enrol in kids programmes? →
Junior Tech Stars: Ages 6–10 (introductory). Digital Skills Accelerator: Ages 12–16 (intermediate to advanced). Both programmes are designed for the specific learning level of each age group.
Do I need prior experience? →
No. All courses start from the basics and build up. Whether you're 6 or 56, complete beginner or slightly advanced, we meet you where you are. Kids programmes assume zero prior knowledge.
Can I attend online if I'm outside Nigeria? →
Yes! We offer live Zoom classes for students anywhere in the world. Same curriculum. Same certificate. Choose online or physical — both are equally respected. See our International & Online Learning page for timezone details.
How do I know the certificate is real? →
Our certificates are signed and verifiable. We issue them after students complete the full curriculum and portfolio project. The certificate includes your name, course title, completion date, and instructor signature. You can share it on LinkedIn, include in your CV, or show employers.
What if I miss a class? →
For online classes, recordings are available for 48 hours. For physical classes, we provide notes and materials. If you know you'll miss multiple days, contact us — we can sometimes arrange a make-up session.
What comes after I finish a course? →
You leave with a portfolio of real projects, a certificate, and ongoing access to our WhatsApp community. We also offer advanced courses and mentorship for graduates who want to continue learning. Many graduates have gone on to freelance or start their own businesses.
Do you offer job placement? →
Not directly. But our professional courses (Virtual Assistant, Web Development, Graphic Design) teach you how to find and land real clients — on platforms like Upwork, Fiverr, and locally. We equip you to work for yourself immediately after graduation.
Can companies hire us for corporate training? →
Yes! We offer customized corporate training packages. Companies can upskill their teams in graphic design, data analysis, cyber security, virtual assistance, and more. Contact us at debarnitechhub@gmail.com for quotes.
